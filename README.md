## Crestline Social

Free, no-login tools for creators, plus the data behind them.

- **[Free creator tools](https://crestlinesocial.com/tools/)** — YouTube rank tracker (signed-out search, 33 countries), live subscriber count, hashtag generators that count what top videos actually use, money calculator, giveaway pickers, thumbnail and transcript downloaders, Instagram username/user-ID checkers.
- **[youtube-hashtag-study](https://github.com/crestlinesocial/youtube-hashtag-study)** — hashtags on 5,416 top YouTube videos across 50 niches (CC BY 4.0) and a small rank-check CLI.
- **[Blog](https://crestlinesocial.com/blog/)** — how YouTube counts views (the 24 Aug 2026 change), what "free views" offers really require, hashtag counts by niche.
